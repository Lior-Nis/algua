  windows.

  positive windows. Measured own breadth: 2.
- Preview gate: binding pass; scratch stage advanced to candidate. Holdout: 584 bars, Sharpe 0.6840,
  return 28.18%, max drawdown -26.92%. PIT, observation-count, and raw-positive-Sharpe checks passed.
  Advisory failures: breadth-adjusted Sharpe (0.6840 < 2.1667 at effective breadth 25), DSR evidence

Verdict: discarded. The raw evidence is stable but not strong enough after the effective funnel
