# Research Run 20260814-150000

## Scope and controls

This run explored a defensive cross-sectional low-volatility thesis: among liquid equities, names
with structurally low trailing realized volatility may earn better risk-adjusted returns than their
high-volatility peers. Three mechanisms were evaluated, none of which duplicates the supplied
recently-tested hypotheses.

Every evaluation used immutable bars snapshot `b1fb94b29fcb7601`, point-in-time universe
`liquid10`, the half-open period `2015-01-01` through `2026-08-14`, four walk-forward windows, a
20% untouched holdout, default 5 bp fees plus 5 bp slippage, `top_k_equal_weight` with `top_k=3`,
and `rank_by=mean_sharpe`. All return and volatility calculations used sorted `adj_close`; raw close
and raw volume were not used. The snapshot passed `algua data verify`. Each strategy declared a
121-bar feature lookback, and no relaxation or human-only flag was used.

## 1. Cross-horizon low-volatility consensus

Strategy: `cross_horizon_low_vol_consensus`

Hypothesis: names that remain low volatility over both an intermediate and a 120-session horizon
should outperform names whose apparent calmness is only short-lived. The signal combines
cross-sectional calmness ranks from the two complete adjusted-return windows.

Final parameters: `short_lookback=90`, `long_lookback=120`, `short_weight=1.0`.

Full PIT backtest:

- Total return `244.65%`, CAGR `11.27%`, Sharpe `0.8133`, annual volatility `14.41%`.
- Maximum drawdown `-22.11%`, turnover `44.50`, and `133` rebalances.

Walk-forward evidence:

- Window Sharpes: `0.8009`, `0.5561`, `1.1548`, `0.1211`.
- Mean Sharpe `0.6582`, minimum Sharpe `0.1211`, and `100%` positive windows.

Measured sweep:

- Exact grid: `short_lookback=[60,90]`, `short_weight=[0.5,1.0]` (`4` combinations).
- The final `90/1.0` configuration ranked first by mean Sharpe at `0.6582`; grid means occupied a
  tight `0.6060` to `0.6582` range.
- The final configuration also kept all four windows positive. Recorded own breadth was `4`.

Scratch preview gate:

- Passed the binding floor and advanced to `candidate` in the scratch registry.
- Holdout: `584` bars from `2024-04-16` through `2026-08-13`, return `47.37%`, Sharpe `1.1915`,
  annual volatility `14.99%`, and maximum drawdown `-12.41%`.
- PIT, observation-count, raw-positive-Sharpe, return, positive-window, minimum-window, and
  idiosyncratic-alpha checks passed. Appraisal ratio was `1.2389`, and all volatility-regime
  Sharpes were positive: `1.6661`, `1.2810`, and `0.8375`.
- Advisory failures were the breadth-deflated Sharpe (`1.1915 < 2.2960` at effective breadth
  `42`), DSR evidence (`0.9342 < 0.95`), and bootstrap confidence (`0.4724 < 0.95`).

Verdict: `candidate-preview-pass`. The independent interpretation call recommended cautious
promotion because the holdout improved on the walk-forward mean, every pre-holdout window and
regime stayed positive, drawdown improved in holdout, and idiosyncratic alpha was strong. The
breadth-adjusted and bootstrap failures keep this a forward-validation candidate, not confirmed
evidence of a durable anomaly.

## 2. Market-residual low volatility

Strategy: `market_residual_low_volatility`

Hypothesis: names with low stock-specific volatility after subtracting each day's equal-weight
cross-sectional market return should outperform high-residual-volatility peers.

Final parameters: `lookback=60`.

Full PIT backtest:

- Total return `242.31%`, CAGR `11.20%`, Sharpe `0.7324`, annual volatility `16.33%`.
- Maximum drawdown `-28.02%`, turnover `131.17`, and `390` rebalances.

Walk-forward evidence:

- Window Sharpes: `0.9499`, `0.9056`, `1.1904`, `0.0756`.
- Mean Sharpe `0.7804`, minimum Sharpe `0.0756`, and `75%` positive-return windows; the final
  window lost `0.41%` despite its slightly positive Sharpe.

Measured sweep:

- Exact grid: `lookback=[60,90,120]` (`3` combinations).
- The final 60-session configuration ranked first by mean Sharpe at `0.7804`. The 120-session
  alternative had lower mean Sharpe (`0.6158`) but better minimum Sharpe (`0.2089`) and all windows
  positive. Recorded own breadth was `3`.

Scratch preview gate:

- Passed the binding floor and advanced to `candidate` in the scratch registry.
- Holdout: `584` bars, return `20.61%`, Sharpe `0.5901`, annual volatility `15.82%`, and maximum
  drawdown `-28.02%`.
- Binding PIT, observation-count, and raw-positive-Sharpe checks passed. Idiosyncratic appraisal
  ratio was `0.4198`; regime Sharpes were `1.6778`, `0.2160`, and `0.2542`.
- Advisory failures were breadth-deflated Sharpe (`0.5901 < 2.2960`), DSR evidence
  (`0.6869 < 0.95`), and bootstrap confidence (`0.1461 < 0.95`).

Verdict: discarded. The interpretation call rejected the scratch pass because the last
walk-forward segment deteriorated, the holdout retained a deep `-28.02%` drawdown, and the holdout
Sharpe fell materially below the walk-forward mean while all three statistical-confidence
advisories failed decisively.

## 3. Robust-dispersion low volatility

Strategy: `robust_dispersion_low_volatility`

Hypothesis: the defensive edge should be more reliable when ordinary realized volatility and
median absolute deviation agree that a name is calm, rather than when isolated shocks determine
the ranking.

Final parameters: `lookback=60`, `mad_weight=0.5`.

Full PIT backtest:

- Total return `152.64%`, CAGR `8.33%`, Sharpe `0.6279`, annual volatility `14.39%`.
- Maximum drawdown `-22.50%`, turnover `141.83`, and `421` rebalances.

Walk-forward evidence:

- Window Sharpes: `0.8702`, `0.5896`, `0.8447`, `0.1861`.
- Mean Sharpe `0.6226`, minimum Sharpe `0.1861`, and `100%` positive windows.

Measured sweep:

- Exact grid: `lookback=[60,90,120]`, `mad_weight=[0.5,1.0]` (`6` combinations).
- The final `60/0.5` configuration ranked first by mean Sharpe at `0.6226` and was the only grid
  point with all four positive windows. Grid means ranged from `0.5627` to `0.6226`; recorded own
  breadth was `6`.

Scratch preview gate:

- Passed the binding floor and advanced to `candidate` in the scratch registry.
- Holdout: `584` bars, return `26.05%`, Sharpe `0.7515`, annual volatility `14.74%`, and maximum
  drawdown `-17.88%`.
- Binding PIT, observation-count, and raw-positive-Sharpe checks passed. Idiosyncratic appraisal
  ratio was `0.7303`; regime Sharpes were `1.8562`, `0.4911`, and `0.2631`.
- Advisory failures were breadth-deflated Sharpe (`0.7515 < 2.3278` at effective breadth `48`),
  DSR evidence (`0.7930 < 0.95`), and bootstrap confidence (`0.2209 < 0.95`).

Verdict: `candidate-preview-pass`. The independent interpretation call recommended cautious
promotion because all four windows were positive, the holdout exceeded the walk-forward mean, and
drawdown improved in holdout. The high turnover, weak worst-window Sharpe, and failed statistical
confidence checks make it a lower-conviction candidate that needs strict forward validation.

## Run decision

Two hypotheses are named for authoritative replay: cross-horizon consensus as the stronger result,
and robust dispersion as a distinct, lower-conviction robustness mechanism. Market-residual low
volatility is discarded despite its scratch binding pass. No strategy was advanced beyond the
scratch `candidate` stage.
