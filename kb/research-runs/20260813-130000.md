## 1. Path Efficiency Quality Momentum

Strategy: `path_efficiency_quality_momentum`

Hypothesis: among names with comparable medium-term skip momentum, adjusted-close paths with higher path efficiency and less underwater time outperform choppier paths.

Signal: ranks skip-month momentum plus a quality rank from trailing path efficiency minus drawdown and underwater-share penalties. Base params: `lookback=252`, `momentum_lookback=126`, `skip=21`, `quality_weight=0.75`; construction `top_k_equal_weight`, `top_k=3`.

Walk-forward: mean Sharpe `0.5395`, min Sharpe `0.1162`, `pct_positive_windows=1.0`. Window total return / Sharpe: `1.04% / 0.1162`, `13.52% / 0.6947`, `8.79% / 0.3581`, `48.00% / 0.9890`.

Sweep: `lookback=[168,252]`, `quality_weight=[0.5,0.75,1.0]`, six combos. Best combo was `lookback=168`, `quality_weight=0.5` with mean Sharpe `0.7905`, min Sharpe `0.4154`; the base config ranked fifth with mean Sharpe `0.5395`.

Preview gate: promoted to `candidate` in the scratch registry. Binding checks passed: PIT, `452` holdout observations, and raw holdout Sharpe `0.6867 > 0`. Holdout return was `18.62%`. Advisory concerns: breadth-deflated holdout Sharpe failed (`0.6867 < 2.1352`), DSR evidence failed (`0.7576 < 0.95`), and DSR bootstrap failed (`0.1808 < 0.95`).

Verdict: candidate-preview-pass. The binding floor passed and every walk-forward window was positive, but this is the weakest candidate of the three because the base params were not near the sweep peak and the first OOS window was thin.
