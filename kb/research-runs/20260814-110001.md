## Lagged rank persistence momentum

Strategy module: `lagged_rank_persistence_momentum`

Hypothesis: a liquid equity with strong 12-1 month adjusted-return momentum is more likely to
continue leading when its cross-sectional percentile rank was also high at the preceding three
monthly anchors. Averaging those four ranks and penalizing their dispersion should demote names
whose apparent leadership is transient.

The signal computes a 252-session adjusted return ending 21 sessions before each decision time at
four anchors spaced 21 sessions apart. It scores each name as its current cross-sectional rank plus
`persistence_weight * (mean_anchor_rank - anchor_rank_std)`. Construction holds the top three names
at equal weight. Base parameters were `formation_lookback=252`, `skip_bars=21`,
`anchor_spacing=21`, `anchor_count=4`, and `persistence_weight=0.75`; the declared feature lookback
was 336 bars and the central execution contract applied the required one-bar decision lag.
